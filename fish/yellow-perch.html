Yellow Perch

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Striped, golden, and delicious perch school tightly, so where you catch one, you'll catch twenty. Arguably the best-eating fish in fresh water.

Where to find them

Approximate locations only. Check local access and current regulations before fishing.

  • Emigrant Reservoir perch schools around structure
  • Agate Lake good numbers
  • Lake of the Woods perch plus trout in a mountain setting

What they eat and the lures that match

Prey When it matters Lure or bait that mimics it
Insects, larvae, and snails All year Worms, small jigs, maggots
Small fish Larger perch Small minnows and jig-and-minnow combos

When they're most active

Season Best time Why
Spring (spawn) Shallow, all day Fish move shallow to spawn; easy limits
Summer Morning Schools hold on drop-offs; find one school and stay on it
Winter Midday, deep Perch bite well in cold water schools sit deep

Regulations

Always verify before you go. Seasons, bag limits, and gear rules change by water and time of year. This page is a guide, not the law check the official ODFW regulations for the water you're fishing.

Rule Typical baseline
