Brown Bullhead

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

The smaller, everywhere cousin of the catfish. Not glamorous, but always willing to bite and a reliable fish for kids on a summer evening.

Where to find them

Approximate locations only. Check local access and current regulations before fishing.

  • Expo Pond bullheads bite all summer
  • Emigrant Reservoir around shallow muddy bottoms
  • Ponds and sloughs everywhere if there's warm still water, there are bullheads

Fun facts

  • Brown bullheads can tolerate warm, low-oxygen water better than many fish.
  • The sharp pectoral and dorsal spines can prick anglers, so handle carefully.
