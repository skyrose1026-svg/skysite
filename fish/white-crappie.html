White Crappie

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

The black crappie's paler cousin look for vertical bars instead of scattered speckles, and count the dorsal spines if you want to be sure.

Where to find them

Approximate locations only. Check local access and current regulations before fishing.

  • Emigrant and Agate reservoirs less common than black crappie locally
  • Murky backwaters and sloughs white crappie tolerate turbid water better

Fun facts

  • White crappie usually show vertical bars and have fewer dorsal-fin spines than black crappie.
  • They often school near brush, docks, and submerged cover.
