Before you head out

Frequently asked questions

A few quick notes about privacy, offline use, and checking the rules.

Are my catch logs private?

Yes. Entries stay in this browser on this device. Keep exported backups private.

Do I need cell service on the water to use this site?

Cached pages and saved notes can work offline. Live gauges, forecasts, maps, and forum updates need internet.

How do live river gauges work offline?

They don’t update offline. Field Tools may show a saved, possibly outdated snapshot; check USGS for current readings.

Do I need a paper fishing license in Oregon?

ODFW offers electronic licensing, but document requirements vary. Check current ODFW rules for your license and tags.
