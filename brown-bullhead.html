Brown Bullhead

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

The smaller, everywhere cousin of the catfish. Not glamorous, but always willing to bite — and a reliable fish for kids on a summer evening.

Where to find them

  • Expo Pond — bullheads bite all summer
  • Emigrant Reservoir — around shallow muddy bottoms
  • Ponds and sloughs everywhere — if there's warm still water, there are bullheads

What they eat — and the lures that match

Prey When it matters Lure or bait that mimics it
Anything smelly on the bottom
Dusk and night
Worms, stinkbait, dough bait, cut fish

When they're most active

Season Best time Why
Spring and summer Dusk and night Bottom feeding peaks in the dark
Winter Slow, midday Deep muddy holes
