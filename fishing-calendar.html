Typical activity windows this month

Highlighted periods describe expected fish activity, migration timing, or common seasonal access—not whether a fishery is open. Dates outside a highlighted period are generally less active for that seasonal pattern; most fish do not become biologically dormant.

Before you go: Fish presence does not mean harvest is allowed. Check current ODFW regulations for the exact water, dates, species, and retention rules. Offshore seasons and quotas can change during the year. Bull trout are intentionally not listed as a target. They have strict protections; check current rules and avoid harming or retaining them.
