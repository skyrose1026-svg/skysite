Rogue River

Southern Oregon

The Rogue is Southern Oregon's flagship river - a 215-mile system with spring and fall chinook, coho, summer and winter steelhead, trout, and a world-class smallmouth bass fishery below Grants Pass. If you learn one river well, make it this one.

Location

Primary access: Grants Pass (Griffins Park)

Tributaries & Nearby Waters

  • Applegate River - joins at Grants Pass; winter steelhead and fall chinook spawning
  • Illinois River - joins at Agness; wild winter steelhead and fall chinook
  • Bear Creek - flows through Medford and Ashland; urban stream, cutthroat and steelhead
  • Evans Creek - joins near Wimer; steelhead and cutthroat
  • Little Butte Creek - joins near Eagle Point; steelhead and cutthroat
  • Big Butte Creek - joins near Trail; wild trout and steelhead
  • Elk Creek - joins near Trail; steelhead and cutthroat (not the coastal Elk River)
  • Trail Creek - joins near Trail; small wild trout stream
  • Mill Creek (upper) - joins above Union Creek; headwater trout
  • Union Creek - joins near Union Creek Resort; headwater trout

Details

TopicInfo
Best waterUpper: Shady Cove to Lost Creek (trout, summer steelhead). Middle: Galice to Agness (chinook, steelhead). Lower: Agness to Gold Beach (fall chinook, jet boat water)
AccessGriffins and Riverside parks in Grants Pass, Galice Resort, Rainie Falls trail, Agness, Lobster Creek, Gold Beach
HazardsDrift boats and jet boats share the water; summer swimmers; cold water year-round
SeasonsSpring chinook May-July, fall chinook Sept-Nov, summer steelhead June-Oct, winter steelhead Dec-April, smallmouth June-Oct
Gear noteDrift boats dominate the middle river; bank anglers do well at Rainie Falls, Lobster Creek, and the Grants Pass parks
