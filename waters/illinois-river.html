Illinois River

Southern Oregon

A wild and scenic river draining the Kalmiopsis wilderness, joining the Rogue at Agness. Wild winter steelhead, fall chinook, and cutthroat in a rugged canyon - this is Oregon's wild-fish water.

Location

Primary access: Illinois River Forks State Park
