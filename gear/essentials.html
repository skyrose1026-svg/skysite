Fishing Trip Essentials

A few simple tools make it easier to land fish safely, handle tackle, and be prepared for changing conditions.

Useful tools and necessities

Landing net

Helps bring a fish under control with less handling. Choose a net large enough for your target fish; a rubberized net can reduce tangles and handling abrasion.

Useful for: trout, bass, and larger fish from shore or boat. Follow fish-handling and release guidance.

Pliers or hemostats

Help remove hooks, pinch barbs when permitted, and cut line. Keep fingers away from hooks and use the right tool for deep or difficult hook removal.

Tackle box or small organizer

Keeps lures, hooks, weights, and spare line sorted. Carry only the tackle you expect to use and secure sharp hooks.

Safety and trip basics

  • Water, weather-appropriate layers, and sun protection.
  • First-aid kit and a charged phone; tell someone where you are going.
  • Waders or a personal flotation device when appropriate; never assume a river is safe to wade.
  • Required fishing license and a way to check current regulations.

For fishing access, safety, and current rules, consult the relevant water information and ODFW regulations.
