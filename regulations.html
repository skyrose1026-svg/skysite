Before every trip

Regulation & access checklist

Use this quick check to find the current official rules for the exact place and date you plan to fish. The guide's species and water pages are informational and do not replace current regulations.

Confirm each detail

  • Exact water and section: identify the river, lake, marine area, and regulation zone.
  • Date and species: check open seasons, emergency updates, closures, size limits, and retention rules.
  • Method and gear: verify legal bait, hooks, tackle, and fishing methods for that water.
  • Access: confirm public access, land-manager restrictions, parking, and any local closures.
  • License and tags: check which current license, tag, or endorsement applies to your trip.

Rules can change during a season. Check the official source again on the day of your trip.
